Why Suraksha Alert is uniquely architected compared to existing safety solutions.

The Core Problem With Existing Apps

There are dozens of SOS panic button apps on the Play Store, yet women in real distress rarely use them. Why? Because when a real threat emerges:

  • The Panic Delay: Victims often have less than 5 seconds. Reaching for a phone, unlocking it, and locating an SOS button is physically prevented by an attacker.
  • The False Sense of Security: Users assume their phone will save them, but their GPS was turned off, emergency contacts were never verified, or battery was low.
  • Broadcast Chaos: Blasting an SMS to 5 friends simultaneously creates duplicate panic where everyone assumes someone else is handling the situation.

Suraksha Alert's Architectural Breakthrough

"Suraksha Alert focuses not only on emergency response, but also on safety preparation before a journey and organised contact escalation when a check-in is missed."

1

1. Pre-Journey Readiness Scoring

Audits contacts, battery, sensors, and GPS before travel so you never step into danger unprotected.

If you are incapacitated, the app alerts contacts automatically when your ETA timer lapses without check-in.

3

3. Acknowledgement Escalation

Enforces a priority ladder: Contact 1 must acknowledge the emergency or it cascades to Contact 2, 3, and 112.

4

4. Non-Intrusive De-escalation

A lifelike Fake Call simulator allows women to escape uncomfortable confrontations without escalating aggression.
